Layers
Pick a layer, choose its effect, then paint where it should move. A layer moves only its own area, at its own Cycles rate; where masks overlap, the top layer wins.
Built-in estimate — instant, no download.
Motion mask
The brush edits whatever is selected here — the layer's motion mask, or an emitter's spawn area, the place its particles are born. Tapping an emitter in Particles switches this to its spawn area as well.
Every tool here edits whatever the Layer picker above points at — the selected layer's motion mask, or an emitter's spawn area — and nothing else.
Particles
Emitters
No emitters yet. Add one to drop flickering embers, smoke, snow or rain over the photo.
Each emitter is a pass of its own over the photo. Its particles are born only inside the spawn area you paint — an emitter with no area shows nothing. Sizes and density follow the frame, so the export matches the preview. Tapping a row (or its Area button) sends the brush to that emitter's spawn area; press Done under the photo to go back to a layer.
